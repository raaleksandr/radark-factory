# Research briefs

One self-contained brief per question: `HR-NNN-<slug>.md`. Index and status: the [register](../README.md).

- The top of a brief (title, register link, date written, what it informs) is for us. Everything **below the marker line** is pasted into the research tool as is, with no other context.
- The pasted part holds:
  - our workflow and setup, enough to judge fit (from [workflow-today.md](../workflow-today.md));
  - the evaluation frame: the `orchestration-harness` requirements, must-haves marked;
  - what is already known or assumed, so the report checks it rather than repeats it;
  - the exact, numbered questions, and what is out of scope;
  - the required output: summary, findings with sources and dates, a table per candidate against the requirements, confidence per finding, gaps;
  - a request to cite sources with links and dates, prefer 2025–2026 sources and primary ones (official docs, terms, repositories, release notes), and tell documented behaviour, reported experience and opinion apart.
- No credentials, keys or private data.

The owner runs the brief and saves the result to [../reports/](../reports/README.md).
